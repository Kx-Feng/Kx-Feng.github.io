## About Me

I am a PhD student at Griffith University working on *watermarking*, *generative AI*, and *AI security*. 
My research is supervised by [Prof. Leo Zhang](https://leozhangcs.github.io/), [Prof. Shirui Pan](https://shiruipan.github.io/), [Dr. Yanjun Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=HKq2LkwAAAAJ), and [Dr. He Zhang](https://sites.google.com/view/hezhang).
Before joining Griffith University, I received my MS in Computer Science from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2023, and my BEc in Financial Engineering from Nankai University in 2020.

</section>

<section id="interests" class="section" markdown="1">

## Research Interests

My research is on **provenance for generative AI**: identifying whether a text, an image, audio, or a video was generated by AI. 
Specifically, I work on **watermarking**, i.e., embedding hidden signals that an algorithm can later extract, addressing the challenges of generation quality, robustness, and security. 
My broader interests include natural language processing, generative models, and AI security.

</section>
